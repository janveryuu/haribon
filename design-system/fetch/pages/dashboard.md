# Fetch dashboard override

The approved visual direction is **Study Fieldbook Next**. This page is an Operate surface for senior high-school through early-college students: calm, clear, and quietly encouraging.

## Visual rules

- Keep the warm light theme as the default: `#F7F9FC` background, `#14213D` ink, `#2F66F6` primary, `#E5F8F2` mastery, and `#EE9D35` meaningful energy.
- Use Manrope for display headings and Inter for supporting UI. Do not switch to Baloo or Comic Neue; they conflict with the approved mature-friendly brief.
- Give the due-study card the strongest hierarchy. Recent decks, creation, tutor, explore, and play remain subordinate.
- Prefer flat, lightly elevated surfaces with 18–22px radii. Avoid generic bento grids, noisy gradients, glow, or decorative grid backgrounds.
- Mascot appears as a selective coach in encouragement or milestone states, never inside the dense study data region.

## Motion

Use 150–300ms ease-out transitions for controls and one restrained entrance stagger for the page. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
